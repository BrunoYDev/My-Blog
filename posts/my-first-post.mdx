## System online. Connection established. Welcome.

You found my little corner of the web. If things look a bit different here, that's on purpose. I'm Bruno, born in 2003, but for some reason I feel more at home with the internet from 1999.

I'm mostly into backend and game dev, so I like building the stuff that runs behind the scenes. This site is where I get to do that and also play around with a visual style I really like: the old web, Y2K, all that.

### So what's this place about?

Basically a mix of things:

* **A Devlog:** I'll post about what I'm working on, what I'm learning, games I'm making. You can find my stuff on [itch.io](https://ykkos2.itch.io/) and [GitHub](https://github.com/brunoydev).
* **A Tribute:** To the time before everything on the internet looked the same. When people had their own weird sites with crazy backgrounds and GIFs everywhere.
* **A Media Journal:** Thoughts on sci-fi (big Doctor Who fan), games, 80s anime, City Pop, that kind of thing.

I write in English here but there's a language selector in the header if you want to read in Portuguese or German. The **Guestbook** takes any language though, go for it.

Thanks for stopping by.
